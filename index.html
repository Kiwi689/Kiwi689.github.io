About Me

A bit about myself

I'm a second-year undergraduate at Southeast University, majoring in Software Engineering. My research interests span world models and federated learning — I'm drawn to questions about how machines can learn robustly under real-world constraints like distributed data and label noise.

I have published work on robust federated learning with noisy labels, and I'm currently exploring the theoretical and practical frontiers of world model research.

Outside of research, I enjoy hiking, playing guitar badly, and reading sci-fi novels that make me question what intelligence even means.

Research

What I think about

Federated Learning

Enabling collaborative training across distributed clients without sharing raw data, with a focus on robustness to label noise and data heterogeneity.

World Models

Building internal simulators that let agents predict, imagine, and plan without interacting with the real world.

Embodied AI

Teaching robots to perceive, reason, and act in unstructured physical environments using learned representations.

Reinforcement Learning

Model-based RL methods that are sample-efficient and generalizable across diverse tasks and domains.
